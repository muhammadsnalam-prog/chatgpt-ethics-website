Homepage / Introduction

What technology did we choose?

Our team chose ChatGPT, a generative artificial intelligence chatbot created by OpenAI. ChatGPT can produce text-based responses, answer questions, summarize information, brainstorm ideas, write code, explain concepts, and assist with many communication tasks.

We chose ChatGPT because it is one of the most recognizable examples of advanced information technology. It is already used by students, teachers, businesses, and everyday users. Because of this, ChatGPT is not only a technical tool. It is also a social tool that affects education, work, trust, creativity, privacy, and responsibility.

Main argument: ChatGPT should support human thinking, not replace human responsibility.
Technology Explanation

How does ChatGPT work?

ChatGPT is powered by a large language model. A language model learns patterns from large amounts of text and then predicts what words are likely to come next in a response. This allows ChatGPT to create answers that sound natural and helpful.

However, ChatGPT does not understand the world the same way a human does. It can produce confident answers that are incomplete, biased, outdated, or incorrect. This is why human review is important, especially in education, business, healthcare, law, and other serious settings.

User asks a question
AI model predicts a response
ChatGPT gives an answer
Human checks and uses it responsibly
ChatGPT is powerful because it is easy to use, but that also makes it risky when users trust it without checking.
Applications of the Technology

Real-world applications of ChatGPT

Our website focuses on two major application areas: education and customer service/business productivity. These examples show how ChatGPT can be useful while also creating ethical concerns.

🎓

Application 1: Education

Students can use ChatGPT to explain difficult topics, summarize readings, brainstorm essay ideas, practice questions, and receive tutoring-style help. Universities may also use AI tools to support students, faculty, researchers, and campus operations.

Benefit: ChatGPT can make learning support faster and more personalized.

Challenge: Students may use it to complete assignments without understanding the material, which can weaken learning and academic honesty.

💬

Application 2: Customer Service & Business

Businesses can use ChatGPT-like tools to answer customer questions, draft responses, summarize customer issues, create internal documents, and help employees work more efficiently.

Benefit: ChatGPT can reduce workload and improve response speed.

Challenge: AI may give wrong answers, mishandle sensitive information, or reduce the need for some human jobs if companies use it carelessly.

Social Challenges

What makes ChatGPT complicated?

ChatGPT is ethically complicated because it can be helpful and harmful at the same time. The same tool that helps a student understand a topic can also help another student cheat. The same tool that helps a business respond faster can also spread wrong information to customers.

Misinformation AI can produce confident but incorrect answers.
Bias AI can reflect unfair patterns from training data.
Privacy Users may enter sensitive personal or business data.
Job Impact Automation can change or reduce some work roles.
Overdependence People may stop practicing their own thinking skills.
Critical Ethical Analysis

Ethical lenses used in our analysis

We analyzed ChatGPT using two ethical lenses: utilitarianism and deontology. These lenses are useful because they look at the technology from different angles. Utilitarianism focuses on outcomes, while deontology focuses on duties, rules, and responsibilities.

⚖️

Lens 1: Utilitarianism

Utilitarianism asks whether a technology creates more overall benefit than harm. From this view, ChatGPT can be ethical if it improves learning, saves time, supports workers, and helps more people access information.

However, if ChatGPT increases cheating, spreads misinformation, violates privacy, or causes harmful job disruption, then the harms may outweigh the benefits.

📜

Lens 2: Deontology

Deontology asks whether people are following moral duties and rules. From this view, students have a duty to be honest, businesses have a duty to avoid misleading customers, and developers have a duty to reduce bias and improve transparency.

Even if ChatGPT creates benefits, it can still be unethical if it is used dishonestly or without respect for privacy, consent, and accountability.

Context Utilitarian Question Deontological Question
Education Does ChatGPT improve learning more than it harms academic honesty? Are students using ChatGPT honestly and following course rules?
Customer Service Does ChatGPT improve service quality more than it risks misinformation? Are companies being transparent when AI is used to communicate with customers?
Privacy Do the productivity benefits outweigh the privacy risks? Are users’ data rights being respected?
Social Implications

Why does this matter socially and politically?

Political influence

ChatGPT can help people understand political issues, but it can also be used to create persuasive misinformation or biased political content.

Economic impact

AI can help workers become more productive, but it may also change job expectations and reduce demand for some repetitive writing or support tasks.

Cultural change

ChatGPT may change how people learn, write, create, and communicate. Society must decide when AI help is useful and when it weakens human creativity or responsibility.

Recommendations

How can ChatGPT be used in a less harmful way?

For schools and teachers

  • Create clear rules explaining when AI use is allowed.
  • Design assignments that require personal reflection, drafts, or oral explanation.
  • Teach students how to verify AI responses instead of blindly copying them.

For students

  • Use ChatGPT as a tutor, not as a replacement for your own work.
  • Do not submit AI-generated writing as if it is fully your own.
  • Ask ChatGPT to quiz you, explain concepts, or help organize ideas.

For businesses

  • Review AI-generated customer responses before relying on them.
  • Avoid entering sensitive customer or company information into unapproved tools.
  • Tell customers when they are interacting with AI.

For developers and policymakers

  • Improve transparency about AI limits and risks.
  • Test AI systems for bias, privacy risks, and unsafe outputs.
  • Create policies for high-risk uses of AI in schools, business, and public services.
Our recommendation is not to ban ChatGPT. Instead, it should be used with clear rules, human oversight, privacy protection, and accountability.

Conclusion

Final takeaway

ChatGPT is a powerful example of advanced information technology. It can support education, improve business communication, and help people work faster. At the same time, it creates serious concerns about misinformation, bias, privacy, academic honesty, job impact, and accountability.

Our conclusion is that ChatGPT should be treated as a tool that supports human judgment, not a tool that replaces it. The future of ChatGPT depends on how responsibly students, schools, businesses, developers, and policymakers choose to use it.

ChatGPT is most ethical when humans stay informed, responsible, and in control.
